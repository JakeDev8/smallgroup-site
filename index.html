Small Group

Bible Check-In

Read the Bible together with your small group. Each day, tap in with a photo of where you're reading, and see who else has.

Coming soon to the App Store

  • Tap in each day

    Take a photo of the page you're reading, add a caption if you like, and post it to one of your groups or all of them at once.

  • See your whole group

    Everyone's posts from all your groups show up in one feed, and each group has a grid of the day's photos.

  • Stay on the same page

    Group leaders can set the week's readings, and a calendar keeps track of the days everyone tapped in.

Got an invite link?

Open it on your iPhone. If Small Group is installed, it takes you straight to the group.

No ads and no tracking. Photos and captions are deleted after 30 days.
